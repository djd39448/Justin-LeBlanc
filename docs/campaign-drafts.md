# Private-event campaign drafts

Drafted 2026-09-21 for review only. Do not publish, connect accounts, spend budget, or send to real
customers from this repository. Round-two requirements remove public demo/package pricing and place
the usable inquiry form in the homepage opening experience. These drafts require a final brand-voice
review before any later campaign approval.

## Celebration

**Landing headline:** Make the room feel like your celebration.

**Landing copy:** Planning a birthday, anniversary, or gathering in the Houston area? Tell me about
the date, setting, and atmosphere you have in mind. The planning form gathers the essentials so we
can talk through the event; sending it does not confirm availability or a performance.

**Facebook / Instagram caption:** A great gathering has its own rhythm. Planning a private
celebration around Houston? Share the date, place, guest count, and feel you want, then review the
details before requesting a consultation. I’ll confirm availability and performance format with
you. **CTA:** Plan your event.

**Tracked URL:**
`/plan-your-event?utm_source={facebook|instagram}&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=private_events_celebration&utm_content=planning_details`

## Corporate reception

**Landing headline:** Live music planned around the room, schedule, and guest experience.

**Landing copy:** Share the event timing, venue constraints, desired atmosphere, and known
production needs in one clear brief. Details that are still open can stay open until we talk.

**Facebook / Instagram caption:** Reception planning has enough moving parts. Use one structured
inquiry to share timing, setting, guest count, production needs, and the budget you have in mind.
I’ll confirm the format, availability, and any quote after we talk. **CTA:** Plan your event.

**Tracked URL:**
`/plan-your-event?utm_source={facebook|instagram}&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=private_events_corporate&utm_content=event_brief`

## Wedding inquiry

**Landing headline:** Music shaped around the moments that matter.

**Landing copy:** Share the ceremony, cocktail, dinner, or reception moments you are considering,
even if timing or production details are still open. Wedding and multi-location requests are routed
to a conversation rather than automatically priced.

**Facebook / Instagram caption:** You do not need every wedding detail settled to start the music
conversation. Share the date or flexible-date choice, city, key moments, and atmosphere. I’ll
confirm availability, format, and any quote after we talk. **CTA:** Plan your event.

**Tracked URL:**
`/plan-your-event?utm_source={facebook|instagram}&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=private_events_wedding&utm_content=key_moments`

## Measurement and privacy

Track only `inquiry_submitted`, `consultation_scheduled`, `hold_created`, and `show_confirmed`, plus
the UTM values above. Do not put names, email addresses, phone numbers, venue notes, free text,
invoice data, or event dates in analytics payloads.
