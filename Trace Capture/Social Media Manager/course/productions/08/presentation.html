LESSON 08

Kickoff, routing, and usage

Direct bounded work and manage model routing and shared usage.

Video · Transcript · Captions

Visual outline
  1. Brief the agent who missed every meeting: A kickoff makes the next safe action findable.
  2. Give each worker a bounded job: Parallel work needs ownership and an agreed interface.
  3. Choose the cheapest capable route: Assign work according to its needs and the available capability.
  4. Monitor the allowance you actually share: Dave’s example is a configurable policy, not a provider rule.
  5. Spend effort once, then preserve it: Track the actual route and reconcile interrupted work.
  6. A compact brief for one worker: A concrete assignment gives parallel work a useful boundary.
  7. Make the kickoff executable: The briefing connects an exact starting point to a bounded outcome.
  8. Separate ownership; share one contract: Parallel workers should not silently invent different interfaces.
  9. Match judgment to the work: Use the available executor that can reliably complete the task.
  10. Watch the dispatch decision change: Illustrative usage under Dave’s configurable reserve policy.
  11. Reconcile before another dispatch: The worker’s actual state matters more than the chat’s silence.
  12. Make worker reports easy to integrate: A consistent reply format keeps attention on consequential work.
  13. Requested is not the same as available: Describe actual capabilities and keep the reporting honest.
