LESSON 10

Capstone: cloud to contract

Assemble and evaluate an agent-ready project package.

Video · Transcript · Captions

Visual outline
  1. Turn your idea into a buildable agreement: The capstone tests how you lead the process, not how quickly you ship an app.
  2. Capture the idea, then agree on the journey: A structured readback turns your raw context into observable behavior.
  3. Research reality and preserve decisions: Your package should distinguish evidence, plans, and uncertainty.
  4. Map the system, then specify behavior: Trace success and failure before turning the design into a contract.
  5. Prepare the kickoff and the long-run limits: Organize work before handing it to an execution agent.
  6. Let a cold reader challenge the package: The reader should not need to reconstruct the original conversation.
  7. Choose a scope you can actually explain: A small project gives the workflow enough substance without unnecessary breadth.
  8. Assess the mirror, not the agent’s confidence: The agreed statement should preserve your intent and reveal suggestions.
  9. Trace the diagram with your finger: A simple diagram should make responsibilities and recovery visible.
  10. Ask how three requirements will be proven: Acceptance criteria should resolve differing interpretations.
  11. Make the next safe action obvious: The final kickoff and handoff must work for a reader who starts cold.
  12. Use review to improve the package: Keep the useful lessons while distinguishing simulation from observation.
  13. Leave the understanding with the work: A transfer note preserves the decisions and identifies what must be rechecked.
