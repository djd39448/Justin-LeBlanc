LESSON 03

Research and decisions

Separate current reality from assumptions and record choices.

Video · Transcript · Captions

Visual outline
  1. Research reality: Check the starting assumption before designing around it.
  2. Four evidence labels: Use four labels that make the strength of each claim visible.
  3. Trace one inquiry: Follow a fictional inquiry without contacting real organizers.
  4. Compare the options: A recommendation should explain the tradeoff behind the choice.
  5. Decision ledger: A decision record carries the reason into the next session.
  6. Turn unknowns into work: Turn uncertainty into a named decision or a bounded research task.
  7. Evidence walkthrough: A code file, local test and live observation answer different questions.
  8. Ask a focused question: Replace “research everything” with questions that can be answered.
  9. Fair comparison: Show both the benefit and the cost of each option.
  10. Owner decisions: Reserve owner attention for choices that materially change the product.
  11. A reusable decision: A decision becomes reusable when its context survives the session.
  12. New facts, new plan: A decision ledger supports learning; it prevents silent changes.
  13. Decide with uncertainty: A provisional choice preserves momentum without pretending certainty.
