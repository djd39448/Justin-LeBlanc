LESSON 04

Architecture and workflows

Map components, information, boundaries, and failure paths.

Video · Transcript · Captions

Visual outline
  1. A map of jobs: Architecture is a map of responsibilities, not a list of software brands.
  2. Follow an inquiry: Follow one inquiry from submission to the owner’s next action.
  3. State over time: A state change needs an actor, a rule and a failure outcome.
  4. Trust boundaries: Keep the responsibility for trust at an explicit system boundary.
  5. Safe retries: Two requests should not accidentally mean two intended effects.
  6. Useful detail: Include the details that let a beginner follow the design.
  7. Draw the system: The organizer and owner sit outside the system; the record connects its parts.
  8. When storage fails: Trace what the user and operator see when storage fails.
  9. Who may act?: Who may act is a concrete architectural responsibility.
  10. One source of truth: A dashboard count is a view of the truth, not a second authority.
  11. Repeat without harm: A retry must account for work that may already have happened.
  12. Test the map: A diagram succeeds when another person can explain what it means.
  13. From map to spec: The specification makes each architectural responsibility precise.
