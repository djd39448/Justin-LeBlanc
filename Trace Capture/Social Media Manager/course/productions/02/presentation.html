LESSON 02

Brain dump to alignment

Capture an idea and confirm an observable readback.

Video · Transcript · Captions

Visual outline
  1. The idea cloud: The same rough request can lead to several very different products.
  2. Ask for a mirror: Turn the brain dump into a small document that can be corrected.
  3. Behavior, not a feature: Show who acts, what they do, and the result they see.
  4. Fact, guess, unknown: A claim’s label matters as much as the claim itself.
  5. The alignment gate: An alignment check is useful while deciding what to build.
  6. A prompt to begin: Use a conversational instruction, not a magic formula.
  7. One conversation: Listen to the whole context before turning a preference into a feature.
  8. Correct the mirror: Make the correction explicit before the wrong interpretation becomes work.
  9. Show the journey: Include the failure path while the journey is still simple.
  10. Keep claims honest: Evidence from one environment does not automatically describe another.
  11. Translate the metaphor: Use a metaphor to discuss the experience, then define its meaning.
  12. Save the readback: The readback is a durable starting point, not a transcript of every turn.
  13. Practice the mirror: Test shared understanding with another listener or your own notes.
