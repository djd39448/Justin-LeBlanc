LESSON 01

The whole system

Explain the conversation and delivery loops and identify alignment and proof.

Video · Transcript · Captions

Visual outline
  1. One messy idea: The approved animated opening is preserved.
  2. Cloud → contract → code: The approved cloud, contract and code sequence is preserved.
  3. Two loops: Two connected loops: agreement guides delivery; new evidence can send us back.
  4. Start with a brain dump: A useful brain dump can contain frustration, examples and unanswered questions.
  5. Mirror the idea: The mirror makes assumptions visible while they are still easy to correct.
  6. The alignment gate: Agreement is a checkpoint, not a reason to restart settled work.
  7. Research reality: Keep observed behavior separate from planned behavior.
  8. Make decisions visible: Compare decisions that actually change the design.
  9. Architecture → specification: Map responsibilities, then attach observable behavior to each boundary.
  10. Authorize the build: A kickoff connects the agreed plan to permission and a starting point.
  11. Build in small slices: Finish one complete, checkable behavior at a time.
  12. Evidence has levels: Each observation supports a specific claim, not every stronger claim.
  13. Your turn: Carry one small idea through the conversation and into a verifiable build.
